# SIGMA redesign — direction and provenance

## Design
Industrial editorial: graphite, molten orange, mineral white, condensed Latin typography, disciplined grid, local vector aluminum material study. Original illustration is a concept, not a product photograph. Product geometry and world network are illustrative. Responsive, keyboard-operated tabs, native modal, native process disclosures, reduced-motion support, and no tracking.

## Verified sources (2026-10-06)
- http://www.sigmacorp.com/cht/intro/intro.aspx — Taiwan founding 1978; nine production bases; annual capacity over 1.2 million tonnes; 100+ alloy specifications; A356.2; 3104/5182; molten aluminum supply; environmental division since 2017; corporate mission.
- http://www.sigmacorp.com/cht/spec/spec.aspx — ADC 3/6/10/12/14; 3104/5182/6063/3033; Zamak-3/5 and ZSG-3. Product specifications are not certification guarantees.
- http://www.sigmacorp.com/cht/product/procedures.aspx — process and molten-aluminum transport.
- http://www.sigmacorp.com/cht/product/quality.aspx — quality checks; certifications mentioned without validity documentation.
- http://www.sigmacorp.com/cht/environmental/eco.aspx — aluminum ash resource recovery.
- http://www.sigmacorp.com/cht/web/contact.aspx — location addresses/phone numbers. Displayed email info@sigmacorp.com differs from original href info@sigmasha.com. This redesign explicitly notes the discrepancy and links to official contacts; email must be confirmed before real use.

All claims are company-reported, not independently audited. No quantitative emissions-reduction claim or certification-validity guarantee has been added. Form generates a selectable local draft only; no mailto recipient, no transmission/storage. Submit is disabled until JavaScript initialization to prevent GET fallback. Site identifies itself as a design demonstration.
